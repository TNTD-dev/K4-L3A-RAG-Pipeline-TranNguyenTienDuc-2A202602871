1. Purpose
This document outlines the criteria required for students to maintain their merit-based scholarships and
financial aid support throughout their academic tenure.
2. Scope
This document applies to all full-time undergraduate students at VinUniversity who receive merit-based
scholarships or financial aid. It details the requirements for maintaining these awards.
• Merit-based Scholarships: Entry merit-based scholarships are awarded for the entire period of
study and are not eligible for scholarship-level upgrades.
• Need-based Financial Aid: Entry need-based financial aid is awarded for the entire duration of
study. Refer to the current guidelines  for financial aid application:  Guidelines for Student
Financial Support Request
• Additional Financial Support: Students may apply for additional financial support from other
sources. This will follow the guidelines or criteria specific to each type of support.
