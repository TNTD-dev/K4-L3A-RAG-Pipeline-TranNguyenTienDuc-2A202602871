I. Scope and Purpose
The Internship Management Policy (the Policy) outlines how internships are managed, supported and
approved by VinUniversity. This policy applies to all full-time students and faculty and staff who are
involved in the internship management process.
II. Policy Principles
2.1. Internships should help students achieve their graduate attributes and provide them with an
opportunity to gain work experience and develop employability skills.
2.2. The University will provide students with the opportunity to participate in internships or
internship-like experiences, and students will be responsible for meeting the requirements.
2.3. Internships are a collaborative arrangement among VinUniversity, Host Organizations, and
students, aligning with VinUniversity's rules, policies, and values.
2.4. Internships must be approved and managed by the Colleges in accordance with this policy to ensure
quality and standard needed to provide a relevant and useful experience with a safe environment
for students. See Appendix 1 for the Internship Approval process.
2.5. Where internships are a mandatory component of a program, this must be clearly stated in the
curriculum framework and listed as part of the program’s inherent requirements.

3.2. Student responsibilities and expectations from University
3.2.1. Students are accountable for their actions and interactions with host organizations. They must adhere
to VinUniversity's Student Code of Conduct, as well as follow the rules and procedures of the host
organization, in addition to fulfilling any legal obligations.
3.2.2. Students participating in VinUni-sponsored or VinUni-facilitated international internships, would be
required to sign a Hold Harmless and Code of Conduct agreement before they depart.
3.2.3. Students are encouraged to disclose to their College (and/or university placement supervisor as
appropriate) any matter that may affect their ability to undertake their internship as soon as it becomes
known. Students are responsible for disclosing any known risks to their safety or the safety of others.
See appendix 3 for Internship Grievance procedure.
3.2.4. Students must be informed/aware of any additional insurance requirements before getting approved
internship.
3.2.5. Students are expected to uphold standards of intellectual honesty and professional conduct. Actions
that could tarnish the University's reputation should be avoided. Disregard for these norms may lead
to disciplinary measures.
3.2.6. Students must follow their host organization's dress code, professional guidelines, and punctuality
rules.
3.3. Health Screening and Immunization Requirements for Clinical Training
3.3.1. Purpose and Scope
This part outlines the health screening, immunization, and infection control requirements for health
sciences students/residents/fellows participating in clinical training at VinUniversity’s affiliated
hospital and healthcare sites. It aims to:
• Protect students, residents, patients, and healthcare workers from preventable communicable
diseases.
• Align VinUniversity’s practices with international guidelines (WHO, NIH, CDC) and national
laws (Law on Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases 2007; Law on Medical Examination
and Treatment 2023).
• Ensure compliance with standards adopted by leading global universities, including strategic
partners such as the University of Pennsylvania.
3.3.2. Health Requirements
• Before commencing clinical training (or by the end of Year 1 for undergraduate students), all
health sciences students/ residents/ fellows must complete the General Health Check-up – as per
VinUniversity admission health requirements and Ministry of Health guidelines.
• Tuberculosis Screening (Tuberculin Skin Test or Interferon-Gamma Release Assay - IGRA) is
strongly recommended.
• Immunization Status Verification – official vaccination record and/or serological testing for:
Hepatitis B (HBV), Measles-Mumps-Rubella (MMR), Varicella (VZV), Pertussis, Polio,
Meningococcus, Tetanus, TB, COVID-19, and Influenza – is strongly recommended.
• Vaccinations for preventable communicable diseases (in case they are not immunized yet) for
HBV, MMR, VZV, pertussis, polio, meningococcus, tetanus, and TB, are strongly recommended.
• Vaccinations against COVID-19 and influenza are recommended, too.
• Bloodborne Pathogen Testing – Hepatitis C (HCV) and HIV testing - before training is strongly
encouraged
3.3.3. Post-Exposure Management
In the event of potential exposure (e.g., needle-stick injury, body fluid contact):
• Report immediately to the Occupational Health Department of the clinical site.

• Inform the VinUniversity Student Health Clinic for follow-up and guidance.
• If exposure occurs on VinUniversity campus, report directly to the Student Health Clinic.
3.3.4. Compliance and Monitoring
• Documentation: Health records will be maintained securely at the VinUniversity Student Clinic.
• Verification: Students/residents are responsible for ensuring their health status remains compliant.
• Ongoing Monitoring: Health status will be reviewed periodically, particularly after any exposure
incidents.
